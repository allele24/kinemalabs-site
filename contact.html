Help

How to start, join and record. For anything else, email alexlee@kinemalabs.tech.

Coaches: start a group

Sign in, open Practice and start a group as coach. Then pick a plan. Coach is $29 a month for one coach with unlimited students. Academy is $79 a month for up to 3 coaches with unlimited students.

Both plans start with 14 days free, then bill monthly. You add a card on Stripe's page when you start and can cancel anytime.

Start 14 days free Try the demo

Coaches: invite students

Kinema gives you an invite code to send to one student. Each code works once and expires in 7 days, so every student gets their own.

Students: join your coach's group

Ask your coach for an invite code. Sign in, open Practice and enter the code. You agree to the terms once when you join.

Sign in

Parents

If the student is under 18, a parent or legal guardian reads the agreement and signs for them. If the student is under 13, Kinema also emails the parent a link, and nothing the student records is sent until the parent confirms.

Recording a set

Put your phone on a tripod behind you, turned sideways, with your whole body in the picture. Record your set and it goes to your coach.

Phones it works on

Kinema works in a phone's browser today. The iPhone app was submitted to the App Store on October 14, 2026 and is in review.

Something isn't working

Email alexlee@kinemalabs.tech with the phone you use, the browser, and what happened.

Don't send passwords, health records or other sensitive information by email.
