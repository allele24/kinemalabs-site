App terms & privacy

The terms and privacy notice the Kinema app shows once at setup, published here so anyone can read them first.

Last updated November 10, 2026 Contact about privacy

Terms of use

Kinema Labs LLC

These terms apply to the Kinema app, including the version that runs in a web browser. Questions: alexlee@kinemalabs.tech. Updated November 10, 2026.

Using Kinema

Use Kinema to record practice and share it with your coach. Creating an account, starting a group or joining one means you agree to these terms. Obtain permission from the people you record and any required parent or guardian authorization. Agreeing on your device does not provide another person’s permission.

Measurements and the assistant

Body tracking and the assistant’s write-ups can be incomplete or wrong. Your coach decides what they mean; the assistant does not coach. Kinema is not a medical diagnosis, injury assessment or a substitute for a qualified professional.

Your recordings

You keep your rights in your recordings. Keep copies of recordings important to you: the video of a whole recording is removed from Kinema’s cloud after 90 days. Agreeing permits processing of the information described in the Privacy Notice to run Kinema and its assistant.

De-identified data

Kinema may remove what identifies a person (names, contact details, faces and voices) and use the de-identified data to run, improve and develop Kinema, including training and testing its models. See the Privacy Notice.

Plans and billing

A coach’s group may need a paid plan. Only a coach can buy one; athletes and parents are not charged. You see the price and agree before any charge. A group’s first plan starts with a 14-day free trial and is then billed monthly through Stripe until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Settings, under Plan: the plan stays open until the end of the period already paid for and is not billed again. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged. There are no refunds for part of a month. If you were charged by mistake, write to alexlee@kinemalabs.tech within 14 days of the charge and it will be refunded.

Your choices and changes

You can turn Kinema’s assistant off in Settings, and delete your account there. Features may change over time. Material changes to these terms or the sharing scope will ask you to agree again.

App privacy notice

Who operates the app

Kinema is operated by Kinema Labs LLC. This notice covers the app, separately from the website. Contact alexlee@kinemalabs.tech or (317) 647-5442 about access, deletion or privacy. Updated November 10, 2026.

What Kinema collects

Your name. The email address you sign in with, when you use Kinema in a web browser. Practice video with its sound, the clips of each swing, and body positions measured from the video. The notes, voice notes, questions and messages you send. For an athlete under 18, the parent’s or guardian’s name, email address, relationship and signature. For a coach who buys a plan, the plan’s status; card details go to Stripe and Kinema never sees them. The network information needed to handle requests.

On your device

On an iPhone, the app stores recordings, video, voice audio, body-tracking results, object observations, notes and replay information on the phone. Camera, microphone and Bluetooth access remain controlled by iOS. In a web browser, Kinema keeps your sign-in, and any recordings still waiting to be sent, in the browser’s storage for the site, and the browser asks before the camera or microphone is used. Kinema sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Body positions are measured from the video on your device; the results are sent with the recording.

Kinema’s assistant

With the assistant on, the text it works from (notes, questions, what was said, drill details, results) and sampled body-joint positions are sent to Kinema’s cloud service, which uses a third-party AI provider (currently through OpenRouter) to write up what was said as messages. Video and voice audio are never sent to the AI provider. Dictation turns speech into text on the phone in the iPhone app; in a web browser it is done by the browser, which may send the audio to the browser’s maker, for example Apple or Google. Practice recordings, clips and voice notes you send to your coach are stored in Kinema’s cloud as the group agreement describes.

Who it is shared with

The coaches in your group see what you send; other athletes in the group do not. These companies hold information only to run Kinema: Cloudflare (stores recordings), Render (runs the service), Supabase (sign-in), Stripe (a coach’s payments), Resend (emails to parents) and OpenRouter (the assistant’s text). Kinema does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Kinema may disclose information when the law requires it.

Service access

The service receives network information needed to handle requests and an access credential for your device or account. Registration, access and usage records can be processed for service operation and security. Session titles, notes or questions may contain identifying information; include only what is needed for coaching.

Retention and deletion

Recordings on your device remain until you delete them, except that the app removes full-quality copies once they are safely in the cloud. In Kinema’s cloud, the video of a whole recording is removed after 90 days; its results, notes and messages stay. Short clips, results and messages are kept while you are in the group. “Delete my account” in Settings removes your account and what you sent; when a group’s only coach deletes their account, the group is deleted and its plan is cancelled. If a coach removes you from a group, what you sent stays with the group until you or the coach ask Kinema to delete it. Turning the assistant off stops future requests but cannot undo earlier processing. Deleting a recording on your device does not itself delete the copy in Kinema’s cloud. Write to alexlee@kinemalabs.tech to have cloud-held information deleted. Each company named above handles information under its own privacy policy.

Children

An athlete under 18 joins only when a parent or guardian enters their details and signs. For an athlete under 13, Kinema also emails the parent or guardian a link to confirm. Until they confirm, Kinema holds only the child’s name, the parent’s or guardian’s details and, in a web browser, the email address used to sign in; no recordings, notes or messages are sent. If they have not confirmed after 30 days and the last emailed link has run out, Kinema removes the child’s account and the parent’s or guardian’s details. A parent or guardian can see what Kinema holds about their child, have it deleted or withdraw consent at any time by writing to alexlee@kinemalabs.tech; Kinema then stops collecting it and deletes it.

Remembered agreement

Your dated agreement is stored, on your device or with your group membership, so Kinema does not ask again per recording or session. Settings lets you turn the assistant off.

De-identified data and product improvement

Kinema may remove what identifies a person, such as names, contact details, faces and voices, and use the de-identified data (for example body-tracking points, swing timings, drill details and note text) to run, improve and develop Kinema, including training and testing its models. It may also combine data into statistics that don’t identify anyone. Kinema does not try to re-identify people from de-identified data.

Other people

Record and analyze other people only with appropriate authorization.

Changes and tracking

When this notice changes, its “Updated” date changes; material changes ask you to agree again. Kinema does not track you across other websites or apps, so it does not respond to a browser’s “Do Not Track” signal.
