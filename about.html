About Kinema Labs

Kinema Labs LLC makes Kinema, which tennis coaches use to review students' practice between lessons and send them notes.

How practice review works today

Students record practice and send the videos by text or email. Coaches scrub through each video to find the swings worth talking about, type the same corrections for different students, and keep track of who practiced in their head.

Kinema replaces that thread. It turns the coach's spoken comments into a written message for the student, and keeps every student's practice, notes and questions in one place.

Kinema does not coach. The coach decides what to say, and nothing reaches a student until the coach taps Send.
