About Kinema Labs

Technology for movement in the real world.

Kinema Labs LLC is a human-movement technology company building toward a practical, sensor-grounded biomechanics platform.

Why we exist

Better movement decisions begin with better access to movement data.

Traditional motion analysis can provide extraordinary detail, but often depends on dedicated spaces, specialized operators, and carefully controlled conditions. Kinema Labs is exploring a more portable path: wearable sensing and offline software designed around the way coaches, athletes, and practitioners already work.

Our aim is to make high-quality movement context more accessible without hiding the limitations of the underlying measurements. We value clear provenance, careful interpretation, and tools that support professional judgment rather than attempt to replace it.

Current

Capture foundation

A Windows desktop prototype for synchronized simulated sensor capture, live telemetry, and local session storage.

Building

Sensor workflows

Real device acquisition, calibration, reliable session management, replay, and movement reconstruction.

Direction

Useful interpretation

Joint-level measures, pressure-informed context, reporting, and feedback designed for performance teams.

Our approach

Build from the signal outward.

We are starting with the less glamorous work—capture integrity, synchronization, data structure, and local reliability—because trustworthy analysis depends on a trustworthy foundation.

The product is not publicly available today. Kinema Labs is actively developing and validating the underlying system before making commercial claims about performance or clinical outcomes.
