About Kinema Labs

Kinema Labs LLC makes Kinema, an iPhone app tennis coaches use to review students' practice between lessons and send them notes.

Why we built it

How practice review works today

Students record practice and send the videos by text or email. Coaches scrub through each video to find the swings worth talking about, type the same corrections for different students, and keep track of who practiced in their head.

Kinema replaces that thread. It turns the coach's spoken comments into written notes, and keeps every student's practice, notes and questions in one place.

Now

iPhone beta

Practice clips, spoken notes with drafts, the student's view of every note, and student questions passed to the coach.

Next

Setup and alerts

A check that the camera is placed correctly before recording, and notifications when a student sends practice or a coach replies.

Later

Other sports

Sports where a coach reviews repeated movements, such as golf and baseball, once tennis works well.
