How it works

From a student's practice to your notes

Practice videos, your notes and students' questions stay together in one app instead of a text or email thread.

  1. 01

    The student records a set

    They prop their iPhone behind and above them with their whole body in the frame, and hit. When they finish, the clip uploads and appears in your Inbox.

  2. 02

    You watch the clip

    Play it at full or quarter speed, step frame by frame, and loop any part you want to look at again.

  3. 03

    You talk, and Kinema drafts the notes

    Tap the mic and say what you saw, for example “finish higher, more hip turn on 3 and 5.” Kinema writes it up as separate notes, each tagged with the swings and the technique point you named. Edit the text with the keyboard, or say a change, like “make the first note shorter.”

  4. 04

    You send, and the student sees it on the clip

    Nothing reaches the student until you tap Send. On their phone, each note opens on the clip and the swings it refers to, with your voice recording attached if you spoke it.
