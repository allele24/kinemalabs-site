How it works

From their practice to your notes.

Practice videos, your notes and students' questions stay together in one app instead of a text or email thread.

  1. 01

    The student records a set

    Phone propped behind them, whole body in frame. The clip lands in your Inbox.

  2. 02

    You watch the clip

    Full or quarter speed, frame by frame, and loop any part.

  3. 03

    You talk, Kinema writes

    Say the short version. Edit the notes by typing, or say what to change.

  4. 04

    You send

    Nothing reaches the student until you tap Send. They see each note on the clip, with your voice.

Details

Before you try it.

What does it cost?

Contact sales for pricing: Alex Lee, Head of Sales, alexlee@kinemalabs.tech.

What do my students need?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later and your group's invite code. They agree to the terms once when they join.

How should students record?

Phone propped behind and above them, with their whole body in the frame.

Do I have to talk?

No. You can type notes, record a voice note that goes to the student as is, or talk and let Kinema write it up.

Which sports?

Tennis.

Where are clips stored?

In Kinema's cloud, for your group only. Details are in the app terms & privacy.
