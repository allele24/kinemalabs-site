How it works

From their practice to your notes.

Practice videos, your notes and students' questions stay together in one app instead of a mountain of email threads and text chats.

  1. 01

    The student records a set

    Phone on a tripod behind them, turned sideways, with their whole body in the frame. The clip lands in your Inbox.

  2. 02

    You watch the clip

    Full or quarter speed, frame by frame, and loop any part.

  3. 03

    You talk, Kinema writes

    Say the short version and Kinema writes one message. Say what to change, or type to fix a word.

  4. 04

    You send

    Nothing reaches the student until you tap Send. Your message opens on the clip it's about. They can tap the swings you named and play your recording.

Details

Before you try it.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on how many students you coach. Contact sales for a quote.

What do my students need?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later and your group's invite code. They agree to the terms once when they join.

How should students record?

Phone on a tripod behind them, turned sideways, with their whole body in the frame.

Can I type instead of talking?

Yes. The message is plain text you can type or edit. Talking is faster: say the short version and Kinema writes it up for you to check.

Which sports?

Tennis.

Where are clips stored?

In Kinema's cloud, for your group only. Details are in the app terms & privacy.
