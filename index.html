How it works

Practice clips, your notes and students' questions stay together in one place, not spread across email threads and text chats.

  1. Step 1

    The student records a set

    Phone on a tripod behind them, turned sideways, with their whole body in the picture. The clip lands in your Inbox.

  2. Step 2

    You watch the clip

    Full or quarter speed, frame by frame, and loop any part.

  3. Step 3

    You talk, Kinema writes

    Say the short version and Kinema writes one message. Say what to change, or type to fix a word.

  4. Step 4

    You send

    Nothing reaches the student until you tap Send. Your message opens on the clip it's about. They can tap the swings you named and play your recording.

Questions

How do I start?

Sign in, open Practice and start a group as coach. Then pick a plan: Coach is $29 a month for one coach, Academy is $79 a month for up to 3 coaches, both with unlimited students. Both start with 14 days free. You add a card when you start and can cancel anytime.

Do I need an iPhone?

No. Kinema works in a phone's browser today. The iPhone app was submitted to the App Store on October 14, 2026 and is in review.

What do my students need?

A phone with a web browser and an invite code from you. Each code works once and expires in 7 days. They agree to the terms once when they join.

How should students record?

Phone on a tripod behind them, turned sideways, with their whole body in the picture.

Can I type instead of talking?

Yes. The message is plain text you can type or edit. Talking is faster: say the short version and Kinema writes it up for you to check.

Does Kinema coach my students?

No. It writes up what you say and passes students' questions to you. Nothing reaches a student until you tap Send.

Which sports?

Tennis.

Where are clips stored?

In Kinema's cloud, for your group only. Details are in the app terms & privacy.
