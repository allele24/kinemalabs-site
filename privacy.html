Privacy Policy

Clear, minimal data practices.

This policy explains how Kinema Labs LLC handles information in connection with this public website.

Effective date August 17, 2026 Contact about privacy

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at kinemalabs.tech, operated by Kinema Labs LLC (“Kinema Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It does not govern future Kinema Labs applications, products, research programs, or services, which may have separate privacy notices before they are made available.

Information this website collects

Kinema Labs does not use advertising pixels, analytics scripts, cookies, contact forms, or user accounts on this website. The pages are static and do not directly collect personal information.

Our hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps. That processing is controlled by the hosting provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, message, and any attachments. We use that information to respond to your inquiry, maintain appropriate business records, protect our rights, and comply with law.

Please do not send health information, biometric data, government identifiers, passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information through ordinary email.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share inquiry information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as email and hosting providers, subject to their applicable terms. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction.

Retention

We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, for legitimate business records, or as required by law. Retention periods vary according to the nature of the inquiry and our legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have sent to us. Some requests may be limited by applicable law, security needs, or recordkeeping obligations. Send requests to contact@kinemalabs.tech with “Privacy” in the subject line.

Children’s privacy

This corporate website is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Security and external links

We use reasonable measures appropriate to this website, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. This website may link to third-party sites; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and change the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated through an additional notice on the website.
